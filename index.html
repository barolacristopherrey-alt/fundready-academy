Home›About

About FundReady Academy

A capability-building fundraising platform for small and medium NGOs who want to fundraise more systematically.

The Core Promise

"In 30 days, your NGO can measurably improve one fundraising capability at a time."

Mission

To help NGOs fundraise effectively by building the core capabilities required to secure sustainable support — through a structured learning and implementation system.

Vision

A region where local NGOs have the capability and independence to mobilise funding confidently, ethically, and consistently.

What FundReady Academy is

FundReady Academy is a capability-building platform — not a course library, not a generic tips blog. We help small NGO teams identify the exact fundraising capabilities they are missing, then build those capabilities one at a time through a repeatable six-step cycle.

Fundraising success is not luck. It is a set of repeatable skills, tools, routines, and assets. FundReady makes those skills learnable, measurable, and executable — even with a small team and limited time.

Who this is for

🏢
Small-to-medium NGOs Local organisations with 2–20 staff raising funds from multiple channels
👤
Executive Directors Leaders responsible for fundraising alongside everything else
📋
Fundraising Officers Part-time or new-to-fundraising staff who need a practical system
👥
Small teams Where fundraising is "everyone's job" and time is always limited

How the platform works

Every capability is improved using the same six-step cycle. You start with an assessment, learn through focused micro-lessons, build real assets using guided workbooks, implement a 30-day action plan, prove your progress, and then re-assess to measure improvement before moving to the next capability.
